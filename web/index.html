🍁 Canadian Space Agency (CSA) & NASA Biodosimetry Alignment

In deep space beyond Earth's magnetosphere, crew members are exposed to constant Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR) and acute Solar Particle Events (SPE). Research from the CSA (EVARM, MOSFET dosimeters, Radi-N bubble detectors, and CANS spectrometers) proves that ionizing radiation causes cumulative DNA double-strand breaks and suppresses bone marrow hematopoiesis.

Emergency Shelter Protocol: When acute dosimeter rates exceed 5.0 mSv/day, astronauts must immediately retreat to the water-shielded storm shelter module and log occupancy until cleared by Ground Control.

Without Earth's gravitational vector, astronauts lose 1–1.5% bone mineral density per month in the femoral neck and lumbar spine, alongside rapid postural muscle atrophy. Daily resistive exercise on ARED (Advanced Resistive Exercise Device) combined with protein-rich nutrition is critical for deep-space mission readiness.

Habitat Environmental Rule (CO2 + Symptoms Synergy)

In microgravity, air pockets without convection cause localized CO2 accumulation around crew work modules. Elevated CO2 directly mimics clinical illness, causing severe frontal headaches, sleep fragmentation, and irritability. ASTRA's habitat rule isolates cabin atmospheric spikes before attributing symptoms to biological pathology.
